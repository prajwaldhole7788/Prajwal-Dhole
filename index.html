01 • ABOUT

What is rainwater harvesting?

Rainwater harvesting is the collection and storage of rainwater from rooftops or other surfaces for useful purposes or groundwater recharge.

🏠

Collect

Rain falling on a suitable roof is directed into gutters and downpipes.

🧹

Filter

Leaves and larger particles can be removed before water enters storage or recharge systems.

💦

Store or Recharge

Collected water can be stored for suitable uses or directed to a recharge structure.

FORMULA PARAMETERS

Why do we use these three parameters?

Every variable in the equation represents a physical constraint needed to convert raw weather data into usable storage volume.

CATCHMENT

Roof Area (m²)

Meaning: The horizontal surface area of the building top that receives rainfall.

Why we use it: Water collection directly scales with catchment area. Doubling the roof size doubles potential capture.

Tip: Measure length × width of the building footprint.
PRECIPITATION

Annual Rainfall (mm)

Meaning: The total height of rainfall that falls in your region over a year.

Why we use it: 1 mm of rain falling on 1 m² of area equals exactly 1 litre of water (1 m² × 0.001 m = 0.001 m³ = 1 L).

Tip: Pune / Maharashtra averages ~750 - 850 mm/year.
EFFICIENCY

Runoff Coefficient (0–1)

Meaning: The ratio of rainwater that flows into the drains versus what gets absorbed, evaporated, or lost.

Why we use it: Roof materials are imperfect. Without this factor, calculations dangerously overestimate real water gain.

Concrete slab: 0.70 – 0.80 | Metal sheet: 0.85 – 0.95
03 • PROCESS

How the system works

A basic rooftop harvesting system follows a simple path.

01

Rooftop

Rain falls on the roof and is collected over the catchment area.

02

Gutter & Downpipe

Water is guided from the roof toward the filter or storage point.

03

Filter

Basic filtration helps remove leaves and larger suspended material.

04

Storage / Recharge

Water is stored in a tank or directed to a suitable recharge structure.

05 • BENEFITS

Why harvest rainwater?

01

Conserve water

Captures rainfall that might otherwise become runoff.

02

Recharge groundwater

Suitable recharge structures can help replenish groundwater.

03

Reduce runoff

Collection can reduce the amount of water flowing over surfaces during rainfall.

04

Build resilience

Stored rainwater can provide an additional water resource when appropriately managed.

06 • CONCLUSION

A small system can make a measurable difference.
